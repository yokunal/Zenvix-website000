n8n Workflow Automation

Connect tools such as spreadsheets, CRMs, payment systems and email to reduce repetitive work.

What the service does

When business data lives in separate silos—such as spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages, payment gateways, and accounting tools—team members spend hours manually copying and pasting information.

Using n8n, an open-source workflow automation platform, Zenvix builds robust back-end connectors that move data securely between your essential business applications. When an event happens in one tool (such as a customer confirming a payment or submitting an inquiry), the workflow executes the necessary next steps automatically.

Practical examples of how a business might use it

Possible integration workflows:

  • Instant spreadsheet sync: Automatically record every new WhatsApp lead and their enquiry details into Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel without manual entry.
  • Payment confirmation alerts: Trigger instant WhatsApp or email receipts to customers as soon as a payment gateway webhook confirms an incoming transaction.
  • Internal team notifications: Send a notification to your operations group whenever an urgent customer request is logged.

Information needed to discuss the setup

To help us architect the appropriate automation pipelines, please tell us:

  1. The software and cloud services you currently use daily (e.g., Google Sheets, Razorpay, Gmail, custom database).
  2. Which manual copy-paste steps consume the most time for your staff.
  3. How frequently the workflow needs to run (real-time webhooks vs. scheduled batch runs).
