About Zenvix Automation

Zenvix is based in Siliguri, West Bengal. The company builds practical automation and software for growing businesses.

Our Approach

At Zenvix Automation, our focus is grounded in practical utility. Growing enterprises often face operational bottlenecks where team members spend hours answering the same introductory customer questions, copying data across spreadsheets, or manually tracking lead follow-ups.

Our approach focuses on reducing repetitive work and making customer communication easier. By developing tailored WhatsApp chatbots, robust n8n workflow automations, lightweight custom CRMs, and clear, mobile-friendly websites, we help business owners establish predictable operational workflows that function reliably around the clock.
