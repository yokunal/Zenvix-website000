SEO (Search Engine Optimisation)

Improve your website’s visibility for relevant searches in your city and beyond.

What the service does

Having a website is only effective if people looking for your services can find it. Search Engine Optimisation (SEO) involves structuring your web pages, metadata, content hierarchy, and technical markup so search engines can clearly understand what your business does and where you operate.

We focus on ethical, transparent on-page and technical fundamentals—such as semantic HTML, accurate meta descriptions, schema markup for local businesses, fast loading times, and mobile responsiveness. We do not make false guarantees or promise specific number-one rankings, as search algorithms and competitors change constantly; instead, we ensure your website follows proven technical best practices to compete effectively.

Practical examples of how a business might use it

Possible application areas:

  • Local search discoverability: Helping a Siliguri or regional business show up clearly when local prospects search for gym memberships, clinics, or repair services in their locality.
  • Service-specific landing pages: Creating distinct, well-tagged pages for individual services so search engines index relevant topics accurately.
  • Technical audits: Identifying and resolving broken links, slow asset delivery, missing alternative text, or unstructured content.

Information needed to discuss the setup

To help us evaluate your current search presence, please share:

  1. Your current website address (if already published).
  2. The key geographic areas you serve (e.g., Siliguri, North Bengal, or pan-India).
  3. The specific services or queries you believe customers use to look for businesses like yours.
