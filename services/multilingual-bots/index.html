REGIONAL LANGUAGE REACH

Multilingual Bots

Help customers communicate in Hindi, Hinglish, Bangla and English.

What the service does

India is linguistic and diverse. In markets across North Bengal, Sikkim, Bihar, Assam, and metro hubs, customers frequently message in a mixture of Roman-script vernacular (Hinglish), formal Hindi, regional Bangla, and English.

Our multilingual bot setups are designed to recognise and respond in the customer’s preferred language style. By understanding colloquial terminology and regional script variations, your automated systems ensure visitors feel understood rather than forced into unnatural English phrasing.

Practical examples of how a business might use it

Possible application scenarios:

  • Local service enquiries: A home improvement contractor or car repair workshop in Siliguri can handle enquiries from Bangla-speaking and Hindi-speaking clients effortlessly.
  • Conversational Hinglish sales chats: A boutique or gym can reply naturally when a member asks: “Hi, gym timings kya hai weekend par?” with appropriate schedule details.
  • Bilingual catalog browsing: A supplier can let users toggle language preferences or simply respond in whatever language the customer initiated the chat with.

Information needed to discuss the setup

To configure multilingual support properly, please prepare:

  1. The key languages your customer base actively uses (e.g., Hindi, Hinglish, Bangla, English).
  2. Key terms, prices, or product names that should remain in their original form without literal machine translation.
  3. Sample conversational transcripts or messages your team typically receives from customers.
